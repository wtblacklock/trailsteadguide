## 2. Fixed

### Factual correction (1 guide, 1 place)

**Acadia's reservation window was stated as 60 days in the one place it mattered most.**
`app/guides/camping-in-the-northeast-for-beginners/page.tsx:247`, in the common-mistakes list,
read:

> Blackwoods and Seawall release 60 days ahead and book in seconds; NH state parks open 11 months out.

No part of that first clause is right, and the same file already said so four times over (lines
132, 188, 335, and the `faqPageGraph` entry at line 67, all "6 months out"). Corroborated from
two independent directions before editing:

- 90% of Blackwoods and Seawall sites are released **six months ahead, on the first of each
  month, at 10 a.m. ET**, with the remaining 10% released **14 days** before arrival on a rolling
  basis ([Acadia camping guide](https://thedyrt.com/guides/np/acadia-national-park-camping-guide);
  [Blackwoods Campground reference](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/maine-national-parks/acadia-national-park-park-at-a-glance/acadia-national-park-camping/acadia-national-park-blackwoods-campground/)).
- Recreation.gov's standard is the 6-month rolling window, with per-facility exceptions including
  short 14/30/90-day windows ([Recreation.gov booking windows](https://outdoorithm.com/booking/recreation-gov)).

Now reads: *"Blackwoods and Seawall release most sites six months ahead, on the first of the
month, and book in seconds; only a small 14-day release is left by then."*

The fix adds specificity rather than retreating from it, and it makes the surrounding paragraph
work for the first time: the mistake being described is "trying to book two months out", and the
14-day second release is precisely what a reader at that point still has. No QuickAnswer, FAQ or
JSON-LD change was needed - those four were already correct, so page and structured data now
agree instead of disagreeing.

### Seasonal is being routed around - now a three-baseline pattern

This is the clearest signal in the catalogue and it is getting stronger, so it is worth stating
precisely. All three guides that landed this cycle are season-shaped:

|---|---|---|
| `camping-after-dark-with-kids` | fall's shrinking daylight | `scenario` |
| `camping-in-bear-country-with-kids` | fall being peak bear activity | `scenario` |
| `how-to-keep-kids-warm-camping` | cold nights | `scenario` |

Seasonal has sat at exactly 4 since the May snapshot while the catalogue went 35 to 61; it is now
6.6% of the guide surface. That is **six consecutive season-shaped guides filed elsewhere** across
three baselines. The category is not short of ideas. Recategorising these three plus the two from
September would take seasonal from 4 to 9 with no new writing, but it is an editorial/taxonomy
call with real URL and internal-link consequences, so nothing was recategorised here.

A related observation: those three guides form an unplanned, coherent cluster -
**camping with kids in adverse fall conditions** (dark, cold, bears) - that is three guides deep
with no hub or browse surface, and `relatedGuides` is doing that work by hand.

## 4. Blocked by sandbox

**Blocked is not clean.** No claim is made in either direction about anything in this section.

Every outbound host this audit needs is refused by the egress proxy with
`gateway answered 403 to CONNECT (policy denial)`. Confirmed this run by direct probe:
`www.nps.gov`, `www.recreation.gov`, `images.unsplash.com` and `www.amazon.com` all return curl
code `000`, and all four plus `m.media-amazon.com` appear in the proxy's own
`recentRelayFailures` log as `connect_rejected`. `WebSearch` works and was the sole research tool
for section 7.

Note for the next run: the brief hardcodes the proxy status endpoint at `127.0.0.1:36667`. The
port is assigned per session and was **38711** this time. Use
`curl -sS "$HTTPS_PROXY/__agentproxy/status"`.

Consequences:

- **414 distinct external URLs unchecked** - 156 Amazon/`amzn.to` affiliate links, 11 nps.gov, 44
  YouTube embeds, the remainder agency/reservation portals and Reddit citations. **Nothing was
  edited or removed on the strength of a failed request.**
- **207 distinct image URLs unchecked** - 93 Unsplash, 78 `m.media-amazon.com`, 30 Wikimedia.
  **No image URL was changed**, and no photo ID was invented.
- **`npm run validate:affiliate` exits 1 with every link reported `[403]`.** These are proxy
  CONNECT denials, not Amazon responses. The script cannot tell the two apart, so **this run says
  nothing about affiliate-link health in either direction.** Do not read it as broken; do not
  read it as verified.

To restore these three checks, the environment's network policy needs `nps.gov`,
`recreation.gov`, the state park agency domains, `images.unsplash.com`, `amazon.com` and
`m.media-amazon.com` allow-listed. Until then this audit is repo-only plus WebSearch.
