### Factual corrections (3)

**1. Recreation.gov release time was wrong site-wide.**
`app/guides/recreation-gov-reservation-strategy/page.tsx` asserted the rolling-window drop as
"7 a.m. Mountain Time / 9 a.m. Eastern" and, in four other places, as "7 a.m. local time".
Recreation.gov's own guidance states: *"Most campgrounds go on sale at 7am PT/8am MT/10am ET, but
not always! Check the campground's Seasons and Fees tab."*
([source](https://www.recreation.gov/articles/list/tips-for-making-campsite-reservations-at-popular-locations/281))
The drop is a single nationwide moment, so "7 a.m. local time" is wrong for everyone outside the
Pacific zone. Corrected in all six places on the page - JSON-LD `faqPageGraph`, `QuickAnswer`
tldr and summary, lede, and the visible FAQ - plus the page `DESCRIPTION` and the `data.ts`
`metaDescription`, which both said "the 7am ET drop".

**2. Colorado guide had a third, different version of the same fact.**
`app/guides/camping-in-colorado-for-beginners/page.tsx` said the Recreation.gov window opens at
"9am Mountain" in two places. Corrected to `8am Mountain`, per the same source.

**3. Florida booking window omitted the non-resident rule.**
`app/guides/camping-in-florida-for-beginners/page.tsx` said "The reservation window is 11 months
out". Florida State Parks grants 11 months to Florida residents and **10 months to everyone
else** ([source](https://www.floridastateparks.org/reservation-information)) - the shorter window
applies to most readers planning a trip *to* Florida. Corrected to state both.

## 3. Flagged, not fixed

### Blocking: external checks could not run (steps 2, 3, and validate:affiliate)

This is the most important item in the report. Every outbound host this audit needs is refused by
the session's egress proxy with `gateway answered 403 to CONNECT (policy denial)`. Confirmed
blocked: `www.nps.gov`, `www.recreation.gov`, `www.reserveamerica.com`, `www.reservecalifornia.com`,
`www.parks.ca.gov`, `wyoparks.wyo.gov`, `images.unsplash.com`, `www.amazon.com`. Both `curl` and
`WebFetch` fail identically, so the brief's "retry once, then confirm via WebFetch" fallback is
not available. Per the proxy README, policy denials must be reported rather than routed around.

Consequences:

- **56 external URLs unchecked.** No claim is made that they are alive *or* dead.
- **73 Unsplash hero images unchecked.** Same. No image was changed; per the brief, inventing a
  replacement photo ID is never acceptable, and none could be verified.
- **`npm run validate:affiliate` exits 1 with 278 "403"s.** These are proxy CONNECT denials, not
  Amazon responses - `amazon.com` appears in the proxy's own rejection log. The script cannot
  distinguish the two, so **this run tells you nothing about affiliate-link health**. Do not read
  the 278 failures as broken links, and equally do not treat the catalogue as verified.

To get these three checks back, the environment's network policy needs `nps.gov`,
`recreation.gov`, the state park agency domains, `images.unsplash.com` and `amazon.com`
allow-listed. Until then this audit is repo-only plus WebSearch.

### Factual claims left alone

- **California federal booking window (`camping-in-california-for-beginners`, lines ~116, ~175).**
  The guide states "National parks: recreation.gov opens 5 months out at 7am Pacific" as a blanket
  rule covering Yosemite, Sequoia, Joshua Tree, Death Valley, Pinnacles, Lassen and Redwood. The
  5-month figure is Yosemite's block-release exception; the site's own
  `recreation-gov-reservation-strategy` guide correctly describes the standard as a 6-month
  rolling window. The blanket claim is therefore probably wrong for most of those parks - but
  confirming each one needs its per-campground Recreation.gov page, which is egress-blocked.
  Left unchanged rather than swapped for something vaguer. **Worth a targeted fix once network
  access is restored.**
- **New York booking window (`camping-in-new-york-for-beginners`).** The guide says only that the
  window "opens well ahead of the date". NYSDEC publishes a specific 9-month window with defined
  release times (8:00 AM weekdays / 9:00 AM weekends, Mar 15 to Labor Day). Nothing here is
  *wrong*, so this is an accuracy upgrade rather than a correction - editorial, not auto-fixed.

## 5. Gap recommendations

Ranked. The first is new from this audit and is the one worth acting on.

1. **Build the canonical reservation-systems reference table.** *(new gap, S effort, high lift)*
   This audit found the same Recreation.gov release time stated three different ways in three
   guides - `7 a.m. Mountain`, `9am Mountain`, and `7am Pacific` - of which only the last matched
   the official source, plus a fourth variant (`7am ET`) in two meta descriptions. That is not
   three typos; it is what happens when 18 location guides each independently restate the same
   external facts. A single sourced table (state, system, URL, window, release time), with the
   guides linking to it instead of repeating it, is both a citable asset and the structural fix.
   Every future state guide compounds this debt.
2. **Guide: "What NOT to bring camping".** *(backlog Tier 1 #6, S effort)* The only unshipped
   Tier 1 content item and the cheapest remaining win.
3. **Seasonal category backfill.** *(new framing, M effort)* Seasonal has sat at 4 guides since
   May while the catalogue grew from 35 to 56 - now 7% of the guide surface. `labor-day-weekend-camping`
   proves the holiday-weekend template works; Memorial Day and July 4 are the obvious siblings.
4. **Second wave of state guides: WA, OR, NC, TN.** *(M effort each)* The region guides already
   link these agencies without a dedicated destination, and the template is well established after
   nine state guides. Do this *after* item 1, so the new guides consume the table rather than
   adding four more copies of the same facts.
5. **Gear bundles to match the catalogue.** *(backlog Tier 2 #12, S-M effort)* The affiliate
   catalogue more than doubled (44 to 93) while bundles stayed at 4, so most of the catalogue has
   no bundle surface. Budget and mid tiers are the natural cuts.
6. **Location index / state picker.** *(new gap, S effort)* 18 location guides with no browse
   surface between the hub and the article.
7. **Fix the California federal-booking claim** once network access is restored - see flagged.

Editorial items deliberately left out of the commit, per the judgment rule: all new content above,
the New York window upgrade, and any Unsplash image replacement.
