## 2. Fixed

### Wyoming state parks require reservations seasonally, not year-round (1 guide, 4 places)

**The finding.** `camping-in-wyoming-for-beginners` asserted a blanket, year-round reservation
requirement for Wyoming state park camping. The requirement is real but **seasonal**. Wyoming
State Parks runs a reservation season of roughly **May 1 through September 30**, with exact dates
varying by park. Outside it, from about **October 1 through the end of April**, most year-round
campgrounds revert to **first-come, first-served and accept no reservations at all**. Cabins and
yurts are the common exception and stay reservable year-round.

Park-level confirmation of the pattern, from the agency's own park pages:

| Park | Rule |
|---|---|
| Curt Gowdy | October 1 to May 14, all sites first-come, first-served; no reservations accepted |
| Keyhole | Reservations required May 1 to Sept 30 (except Wind Creek); outside those dates all sites first-come. Tatanka cabins reservable year-round |
| Glendo | October 1 to April 30, all sites first-come only; yurts reservable year-round |
| Sinks Canyon | All campsites and Sawmill Shelter first-come through April 30, 2027; yurts on the reservation system |

The guide asserted the absolute version in **four** places:

| Line | Surface | Was |
|---|---|---|
| 70 | `faqPageGraph` JSON-LD | "Yes. Wyoming State Parks moved to a reservation requirement for camping, so the old plan of driving up and finding a first-come site at a state park no longer works." |
| 175 | Body, "Wyoming state parks" | "Note that camping at Wyoming state parks now requires a reservation, so the drive-up-and-hope approach no longer applies." |
| 238 | Body, common-mistakes list | "**Assuming you can grab a first-come state park site.** Wyoming state parks now require camping reservations through reserve.wyoming.gov." |
| 313 | Visible FAQ | "Yes. Camping now requires a reservation through reserve.wyoming.gov, plus a park pass." |

All four now state the season, name the roughly May 1 to September 30 window, say what happens
outside it, note the cabin and yurt exception, and tell the reader to check their specific park's
dates. Page and structured data agree. The `tldr` QuickAnswer needed no change: it covers season,
Yellowstone and Teton booking and bear safety, and never mentions state park reservations.

**Why this matters now rather than in spring.** It is November 9. Wyoming's reservation season
ended about five weeks ago, so the guide is currently wrong about the live state of the system,
and it is wrong in the direction that costs a reader a trip they could have taken. The guide
itself flags that "lower-elevation state parks in the east and the Bighorn Basin have a longer
season on both ends" - exactly the reader most likely to be planning a shoulder-season Wyoming
state park trip right now, and exactly the reader told that drive-up camping "no longer applies."

**The fix adds specificity rather than retreating from it**, as the brief requires: it replaces
one unqualified sentence with a named date range, a named off-season rule, and a named exception.

**Corroboration.** Two independent searches, returning the statewide May-to-September pattern plus
four distinct official `wyoparks.wyo.gov` park pages quoting their own dates, and a secondary
state-park roundup agreeing. **No outbound link was edited**, and the guide's existing
`reserve.wyoming.gov` reference was confirmed correct - one secondary source claimed Wyoming uses
ReserveAmerica, which is outdated, and was disregarded.

- [Wyoming State Parks - Reservations](https://wyoparks.wyo.gov/index.php/permits-reservations/reservations)
- [Curt Gowdy reservations](https://wyoparks.wyo.gov/index.php/reservations-curt-gowdy)
- [Glendo reservations](https://wyoparks.wyo.gov/index.php/reservations-glendo)
- [Keyhole campsites](https://wyoparks.wyo.gov/index.php/activities-amenities-keyhole/campsites-for-rvs-keyhole)
- [Sinks Canyon campsites](https://wyoparks.wyo.gov/index.php/activities-amenities-sinks-canyon/campsites-for-rvs-sinks-canyon)

**What was deliberately not asserted.** One source gives booking windows of 120 days for residents
and 113 days for non-residents. That appeared in a single secondary result and could not be
corroborated, so no window figure was added. Flagged in section 3 instead.

**Containment checked.** No other file on the site asserts a Wyoming state park reservation rule,
so four edits in one file is the whole fix.

### New: `seasonal` is now majority commerce, while the winter content sits elsewhere

The most interesting structural finding this cycle. Seasonal doubled from 7 to 14, and **six of
the seven additions are gift guides**: `best-camping-gifts-for-families`, `camping-gifts-for-kids`,
`camping-gifts-under-50`, `camping-stocking-stuffers`, `gifts-for-new-campers`, and
`black-friday-camping-gear`. Only `warm-winter-camping-destinations` is seasonal camping guidance.

In the same cycle, **six genuinely seasonal winter guides shipped into other categories**:
`winter-camping-with-dogs`, `winter-break-camping-with-kids`,
`how-to-dress-kids-for-cold-weather-camping`, `hypothermia-and-frostbite-in-kids` and
`how-to-sleep-in-your-car-camping` into `scenario`, and the campfire/dishes set into `basics`.

So the one category named for time of year is now half commerce, and the seasonal content is
filed under scenario. This supersedes the older, narrower "routing is inconsistent" note that has
been carried since 2026-10-05 - it is no longer two stray guides, it is the category's majority.

Two options are written into the backlog, both of which are a `category` field edit in
`lib/guides/data.ts` plus whatever the browse surfaces key off, not new content:

1. **Split commerce into its own `gifts`/`deals` category.** Seven pages is past the point where
   it reads as a seasonal topic, and gift guides have a different update cadence, a different
   conversion goal and a hard annual expiry that seasonal evergreen content does not.
2. **Re-route the winter set into seasonal** and accept it as the umbrella for both. Cheaper, but
   leaves `black-friday-camping-gear` filed beside `winter-camping-for-beginners`.

Taxonomy is an editorial judgment with routing consequences, so it is **not** in this branch.
Worth settling before the next gift cycle doubles the set again.

The flag is the hardcoded year, not the facts. The moment Glacier publishes its 2027 system, four
surfaces in this file go stale at once, and the guide will be read by people planning summer 2027

### New: guide-level `metaTitle` and `metaDescription` are consumed by nothing

Surfaced while measuring metadata independently. `Guide.metaTitle` and `Guide.metaDescription` are
declared in `lib/guides/types.ts`, populated across `lib/guides/data.ts`, and enforced by
`lib/guides/__tests__/seo-meta-limits.test.ts` - but **no code reads them**. A grep across `app/`
and `lib/` finds exactly one consumer of a `metaTitle`, and it is `GUIDE_CATEGORIES.metaTitle` in

a judgment call about the data model, not an audit fix.
