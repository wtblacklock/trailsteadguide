## 2. Fixed

### Yellowstone's campground booking split ends with the 2027 season (1 guide, 4 places)

**The finding.** On **2026-10-04**, Yellowstone National Park Lodges (the Xanterra-run
concessioner) announced that five campgrounds move their reservations to Recreation.gov starting
with the **2027 season**: **Bridge Bay, Canyon, Grant Village, Madison, and Fishing Bridge RV**.
The concessioner continues to operate them on the ground; only the booking channel changes. The
practical consequence for a reader is the window: those five drop from the concessioner's much
longer lead time to Recreation.gov's **rolling six months**, and campers can now select
individual sites rather than being assigned one.

`camping-in-wyoming-for-beginners` asserted the superseded two-channel split in **four** places:

| Line | Surface | Was |
|---|---|---|
| 54 | `faqPageGraph` JSON-LD | "Yellowstone uses two systems ... several ... booked through them, while the others are on recreation.gov" |
| 94 | `QuickAnswer` summary | "Yellowstone campgrounds are split between the park concessioner and recreation.gov" |
| 180 | Body, "Yellowstone and Grand Teton" | "splits its campgrounds between two booking channels ... Check nps.gov/yell for which campground uses which system" |
| 297 | Visible FAQ | "Yellowstone splits between the park concessioner and recreation.gov depending on the campground" |

All four now state the 2027 change, name the five campgrounds, keep the concessioner's continuing
operational role, and give the six-month window. Page and structured data agree.

**Why this needed fixing now rather than in the 2027 content pass.** A six-month rolling window
on a late-May 2027 arrival opens around late November 2026 - roughly a month from today. The
guide explicitly tells readers to "treat the day your window opens as a scheduled task." A reader
following that instruction this month would have watched the wrong system and the wrong date.
This is the narrow window in which the error actually costs someone a campsite.

**Corroboration.** Three independent searches returned a consistent account: the concessioner's
own camping pages (authoritative for its own campgrounds, and the origin of the notice), plus
independent secondary reporting and two large RV/travel community threads discussing the change
and its shortened window.

- [Yellowstone National Park Lodges - Camping](https://www.yellowstonenationalparklodges.com/stay/camping/)
- [Yellowstone Camping Reservations: The 2027 Booking Change (PageDog)](https://www.pagedog.app/blog/yellowstone-camping-reservations)
- [iRV2 - Yellowstone Campground Reservations Moving to Recreation.gov](https://www.irv2.com/threads/yellowstone-campground-reservations-moving-to-recreation-gov.2195478/)
- [Tripadvisor - YNP Campground Reservation Change for 2027](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g60999-i481-k15528255-YNP_Campground_Reservation_Change_for_2027-Yellowstone_National_Park_Wyoming.html)

**What was deliberately not asserted.** Sources repeatedly give "up to 13 months" as the old
concessioner window, but at least one explicitly warns that the 13-month figure is the *lodging*
rule rather than the camping rule, and the two now differ. The edit therefore says "the much
longer lead time the concessioner allowed" and does not pin a number. Per-campground 2027 season
dates also differ between sources (Bridge Bay May 21 to Sep 13, Canyon May 28 to Sep 19, both
marked subject to change), so no season dates were added; the guide keeps its existing pointer to
`nps.gov/yell` for current dates. **No outbound link was edited.** The fix adds specificity
rather than retreating from it, as the brief requires.

`recreation-gov-reservation-strategy` needs no change: its booking-window table already lists
Yellowstone in the "National Park ... 5-6 months out ... recreation.gov" row, which the change
makes *more* accurate, not less.
